DESERT AND JUNGLE TILE SHEETS
Each prepared atlas is 512x512 pixels with a 4x4 grid of 128x128 tiles. No separation or margin. Individually named tile PNGs are included.
Rows, left to right:
1. Plain ground, scattered ground, textured ground, worn ground

Rotate corner, straight wall and road tiles to create additional directions.
